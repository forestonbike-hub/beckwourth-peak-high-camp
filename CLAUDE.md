# Beckwourth Peak High Camp

## About This Project
Website and business operations for Beckwourth Peak High Camp, an off-grid glamping property on 60 acres near Beckwourth Peak in the Lost Sierra. Located 6 minutes from Portola, CA.

Site: https://beckwourthpeakhighcamp.com
Booking: ResNexus (external)
Instagram: @beckwourthpeakhighcamp

## The Business
High Camp offers glamping accommodations (two cabins, a yurt, and a campsite) for bikers, hikers, and families. Key draws: Beckwourth Peak Trail access, Lost Sierra gravel biking, 20-basket disc golf course, mountain views, dark skies, and a communal atmosphere with shared bathhouse, fire pit, and deck.

The property is off-grid with Starlink wifi, well water, and seasonal operation (roughly May through October).

## Brand Voice
- Adventurous but approachable. Not extreme sports bro energy. Think: welcoming, outdoorsy, relaxed
- Speak to mountain bikers, gravel riders, bikepackers, hikers, and families equally
- Highlight the "Lost Sierra" identity. This area is uncrowded and under-the-radar compared to Tahoe
- Mention proximity to Portola and Reno when relevant for context

## Current Tech
- WordPress site (considering migration to Astro in the future)
- ResNexus for booking

## Content Priorities
- Trail and route information (bikepacking, gravel, MTB, hiking)
- Lodging details and seasonal updates
- Events (e.g., Gravel Social, Lost and Found festival proximity)
- SEO for Lost Sierra outdoor recreation searches

## Things to Know
- CABDR-North alternate route runs through/near the property. This is a draw for adventure motorcycle and dual-sport riders
- The Lost and Found gravel festival beer tents are about 1 mile downhill from High Camp
- No pets, no smoking on property
- Check-in 3pm, check-out 11am
- Cancellation: 14+ days = refund minus 5% fee, under 14 days = no refund
